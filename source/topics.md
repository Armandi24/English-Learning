# Topic bank

Pull from here at `prep`. Tied to his actual life — architecture, BIM/Revit, freelancing,
AI agents, 3D web visualization, cosmology, hiking, tech reading.

Mark a topic `[used: YYYY-MM-DD]` after a session. Reuse is fine after a few weeks —
the second run is usually much better, and that improvement is itself data.

---

## Roleplay scenes

1. **Client design presentation.** You present a design concept; I'm the client with objections about cost and timeline. I don't love the first idea.
2. **Explain a Revit/BIM workflow** to a non-technical client who keeps saying "sorry, what does that mean?"
3. **Freelance contract negotiation.** Price, deadline, scope creep. I push for more work at the same price.
4. **Job interview — freelance AI-agent developer.** Six real questions, real interviewer reactions.
5. **Job interview — 3D web visualization.** Portfolio walkthrough; I ask what *you* actually built vs. what the team built.
6. **Difficult client email, spoken.** A project is late. You call to explain. I'm annoyed.
7. **Onboarding a new collaborator** — explain how you work, your tools, your process.
8. **Pitching a tool you built** to someone who doesn't see why they'd need it.
