# References — listening & shadowing sources

Shadowing needs **real audio**, which this chat can't provide. This is where the actual
material lives. Fill it in as you find things that work; delete what you never use.

**The rule for choosing material:** aim to understand **70-80% on first listen**.
If you understand 95%, it's too easy to grow from. If under 50%, you're just enduring noise.

---

## Shadowing sources

Short clips, clear speech, 1-2 sentences at a time. Repeat ~1-2 seconds behind the
speaker, copying **rhythm and stress** — not just the words. ~10 min a day.

| Source | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| BBC "6 Minute English" | B1-B2 | Transcripts available. Good starting point. |
| "The English We Speak" (BBC) | B1-B2 | Idioms specifically — fills a known B1 gap. |
| British Council LearnEnglish Podcast | B1-B2 | Structured, transcripts. |
