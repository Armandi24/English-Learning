# Phrase bank — chunks & collocations

The core B1→B2 lever. Fluency comes from a store of ready-made **phrases**, not more
single words. B2 speakers don't know more vocabulary than B1 speakers so much as they
know more chunks — and can deploy them without assembling them.

**A chunk is learned after 3+ uses in NEW sentences of your own.** Not recognized. Used.

---

## Review scheduling (FSRS logic, no software)

- Got it right, used it naturally → push the next review **further out**
- Failed to recall it, or used it wrongly → bring it back **sooner**

Suggested ladder: `next day → +3 days → +1 week → +3 weeks → retired`

At `review`, drill only what's **due**. Retrieval first — never show the phrase before asking for it.
