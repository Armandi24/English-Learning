What to do, every day

1

Prep

Here, in Cowork — 1 minute

Just say "prep." A short brief gets written for you — today's topic, today's method, what to target, a phrase or two due for review. Copy it.

2

Talk

In the Claude Project, voice mode — ~45 min

Paste the brief in and have the real conversation — roleplay, debate, a fluency drill, whatever it picked. At the end you'll get a summary block to copy.

No time for voice? You can also just say "prep" and "let's practice" in one place — laptop or iPad — and do the whole session as text. See the Where to practice tab.

Busy day? 15–20 min is fine Short beats skipped
3

Debrief

Back here — 1 minute

Paste that summary block and say "debrief." Everything gets logged and you get one real strength and one real weakness back — no padding.

and, separately
☺

Solo routine

On your own — 10 min

Say "solo" in the folder. You get today's pick in a few lines — one podcast episode at your level, a short clip to shadow, today's drill, and 3 phrases. Chosen fresh each time from what you're actually getting wrong.

  • 5 min speaking drill — shadowing, read-aloud, record-and-compare, or just talking to yourself. It rotates; the pick tells you which one today.
  • 3 min phrases — the 3 it gave you, each in a new sentence about your own life.
  • 2 min listening — anything you enjoy, no effort required.

An email lands at 8:00 AM Tehran every day with today's phrases and one mistake to watch — that's your reminder.

Weekly — say "weekly": a short graded test, closed-book, no hints. It quizzes you on your own error log and phrases, then gives a score that goes on the record. This is the part that makes you practise the hard bits.

Every 2–4 weeks: say "assess" for an honest, unaided check of where you actually are.
Everything else — logging, tracking, deciding what to practice next — isn't on you.
Laptop · iPad · Voice

Where to practice

The files live in two mirrored places: Google Drive on the laptop, and GitHub (Armandi24/English-Learning) for the iPad. They stay identical because "prep" pulls the latest before every session and "debrief" pushes it back after. You don't run any of that by hand.
A

Text, on the laptop

Cowork — simplest

Say "prep", then "let's practice". The whole session and the debrief happen right here. Nothing to copy or paste.

B

Voice, in the Claude Project

claude.ai — ~45 min spoken

Prep here → copy the brief. Talk in the Project (voice) → paste the brief, have the conversation, copy the summary block. Debrief here → paste it back, say "debrief".

Voice always starts and ends on a device with the files — the Project itself saves nothing.

C

Text, on the iPad

Claude app → Code tab — needs Pro or Max

One-time: open the Claude app, tap Code, connect GitHub, pick English-Learning.

Each session: Code tab → choose the repo and the main branch → say "prep", practise, say "debrief". It makes a fresh copy of the files itself, so there's no manual "pull".

At the end it saves to a branch, not main. Tap Create PR, then merge it on GitHub — one tap. Your next laptop "prep" pulls that work down.

Two rules that keep the copies from tangling:
  • Wait for Google Drive to finish syncing before starting on the laptop.
  • One device at a time — never practise on the laptop and iPad at once.

Switched devices and just want to catch up without a session? On the laptop, say "sync".
Same command words everywhere. Only the last step differs: laptop saves to main; iPad goes through a one-tap PR merge.
One-time, then background

Setting up the Project chat

Two Claudes, one system. Cowork (here) has the live files but no real-time voice. The Claude Project on claude.ai has voice but no access to these files — it only knows what gets pasted into it. So: Cowork prepares, the Project talks, Cowork records.
1

Create the Project

claude.ai → Projects → Create Project

Name it something like "English Practice — B1 to B2."

2

Paste the instructions

Project settings → Instructions

Open project-chat/project-instructions.md in this folder, copy the whole file, paste it into the Project's custom instructions field. That is the talk-side rulebook — how to speak, how to correct, the "do NOT" list, the 45-min shape.

Do not paste CLAUDE.md here. That file is the orchestrator's manual — the command table, git sync, the file-update steps. Give it to the Project and the Project starts impersonating the file-side assistant: it talks about debrief and logging, and it invents claims about files it cannot see.

3

Add knowledge (optional, recommended)

Project settings → Knowledge

Upload these so the Project has real context instead of guessing:

method.md tips.md topics.md error-log.md phrase-bank.md

method.md, tips.md, topics.md are static — upload once. error-log.md and phrase-bank.md change after every debrief, so re-upload those two every few weeks or the Project starts correcting mistakes you've already fixed.

Not needed here: notes.md, progress.md, strengths.md, references.md, research-plan.md — those stay in Cowork, the Project doesn't need them to run a session.

The loop

Same three steps as the Daily routine tab

Prep here → say "prep," copy the brief it writes.
Talk there → open the Project, tap the voice icon, paste the brief, have the conversation, copy the end-summary block it gives you.
Debrief here → paste that block back, say "debrief."

Skipping debrief isn't a small thing — the Project remembers nothing between chats, so this folder is the only place anything persists. Skip it and next time's prep is working blind.
